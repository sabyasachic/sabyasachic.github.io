Overview

My research lies broadly in nonparametric statistics, machine learning, high-dimensional statistics, statistical learning theory, applied probability, sequential decision making, and statistical information theory.

A central theme of my work is the development of statistically adaptive methods for estimating structured objects from noisy data. I am especially interested in procedures that adapt automatically to unknown local structure, such as smoothness, sparsity, monotonicity, change-points, bounded variation, or other shape constraints.

Total Variation Denoising and Trend Filtering

A major part of my recent work concerns total variation denoising, trend filtering, fused lasso methods, and related locally adaptive nonparametric regression procedures.

This line of work studies both global and pointwise statistical guarantees, including adaptive risk bounds, element-wise estimation error, and new min-max representations for regularized estimators. These results aim to explain how such methods adapt to local structure in the underlying signal.

Representative topics include:

  • Adaptive risk bounds for total variation denoising and trend filtering.
  • Pointwise estimation error for generalized fused lasso.
  • New min-max characterizations of total variation denoising.
  • Quantile trend filtering and quantile total variation denoising.
  • Locally adaptive regression methods such as LASER.

Shape-Constrained Inference

Another major theme of my research is shape-constrained statistical inference. This includes isotonic regression, unimodal regression, concave regression, matrix estimation under monotonicity constraints, and estimation problems on graphs and tournaments.

The goal is to understand how qualitative structural assumptions can lead to adaptive estimators with strong statistical guarantees, even in complex or high-dimensional settings.

Representative topics include:

  • Risk bounds in isotonic and shape-restricted regression.
  • Isotonic regression in general dimensions.
  • Matrix estimation under monotonicity constraints.
  • Adaptive risk bounds in unimodal and concave regression.
  • Estimation in tournaments and graphs under monotonicity constraints.

Adaptive Nonparametric Estimation

I am broadly interested in nonparametric methods that adapt to unknown structure in the data. This includes decision-tree based methods, dyadic CART, locally adaptive polynomial regression, bounded variation methods, and multivariate piecewise polynomial estimation.

A recurring question is how to design methods that are both computationally tractable and statistically adaptive, especially when the underlying function has spatially varying complexity.

Quantile and Distributional Regression

Part of my recent work studies quantile regression, quantile trend filtering, dyadic CART for quantile estimation, and distributional regression.

These projects are motivated by the fact that in many applications it is not enough to estimate only the conditional mean. Quantiles and conditional distributions provide a richer description of uncertainty and heterogeneity in the response.

Online Learning and Sequential Prediction

I also work on problems in online learning and sequential decision making, including spatially adaptive online prediction, contextual bandits, and prediction under structural constraints.

This line of work connects statistical estimation, adaptive confidence sets, regret minimization, and sequential prediction.
