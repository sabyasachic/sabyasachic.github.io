About Me

I am an Associate Professor in the Department of Statistics at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign.

My research focuses on developing locally adaptive methods for nonparametric estimation and understanding their statistical properties. Recent work includes total variation denoising, trend filtering, shape-constrained inference, quantile regression, distributional regression, and online prediction.

Much of my work is motivated by the question of how statistical procedures can automatically adapt to unknown local structure in data.
