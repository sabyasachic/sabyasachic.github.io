About Me

I am an Associate Professor in the Department of Statistics at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign.

My research interests lie broadly in nonparametric statistics, machine learning, high-dimensional statistics, statistical learning theory, applied probability, and sequential decision making.

Much of my recent work focuses on locally adaptive methods for nonparametric estimation, including total variation denoising, trend filtering, shape-constrained inference, quantile regression, distributional regression, and online learning.

For more information, please see my research page, publications, and curriculum vitae.

Research Highlights

Current research themes include:

  • Total variation denoising and trend filtering
  • Shape-constrained and adaptive estimation
  • Quantile and distributional regression
  • High-dimensional probability and learning theory
  • Online learning and sequential decision making
