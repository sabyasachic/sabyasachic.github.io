Course Syllabus

Course Description

Time series and other sequential datasets consist of observations recorded in temporal order. This course introduces statistical methods for analyzing such data, with an emphasis on developing mathematical models that provide plausible descriptions of observed time series.

We will study multiple linear regression, nonlinear regression, regularized high-dimensional regression, variance modeling, spectral analysis, lagged regression, ARIMA models, and recurrent neural networks. Throughout the course, these methods will be illustrated using real datasets and computational examples.

Course Topics

  • Multiple Linear Regression: Regression over time, trend and seasonal covariates, prediction, frequentist inference, and Bayesian inference.
  • Nonlinear Regression: Piecewise-linear models, changing growth rates, nonlinear periodic models, and estimation of periodicity.
  • High-Dimensional Regression: Regression with many time-dependent covariates, ridge regression, lasso, and regularization.
  • Variance Modeling and Spectral Analysis: Volatility modeling, Fourier frequencies, discrete Fourier transforms, periodograms, and applications to financial and scientific data.
  • Lagged Regression and ARIMA Models: Autoregression, lagged predictors, ARIMA modeling, and forecasting.
  • Recurrent Neural Networks: Nonlinear autoregression, recurrent neural networks, long short-term memory networks, and gated recurrent units.
